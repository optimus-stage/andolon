Andolon is a single-player march game. Gather a crowd in white, get through the police barricades, and hold the কেন্দ্রীয় শহীদ মিনার (Central Shaheed Minar) with 15 people for 10 seconds.

How to play
  • Move the mouse to walk. Walk into bystanders to bring them along.
  • Police barricades (6 on the outer ring, 2 on each inner ring) open when your crowd is big enough: 6, then 14, then 25 people. Stand near one and wait.
  • Police (blue) carry sticks, in every zone. Each hit costs you morale. If they hit you when your morale is nearly gone, a police van takes you to a thana or Aynaghor and the game is over.
  • Hospitals restore your morale after a beating. বিরিয়ানি stalls restore morale too, and মামার টঙের চা gives a quick boost.
  • Rivals (black clothes) support the police and fight your crowd when you meet. The bigger crowd wins the clash, and a tie goes to the protesters. Some beaten rivals switch to your side. A bigger rival crowd standing in the square stops you holding it. Expect gunshot and blast sounds during fights.
  • Buildings are solid, so walk around them on the roads.
  • You have 30 days (about 4 minutes). Your score comes from crowd size, barricades passed, fights won and speed, and your card shows a rank and a tag. If time runs out you get a friendly roast. Press M (or tap the speaker) to mute sound. On a phone, touch and hold a direction on the round pad at the bottom right to walk.
How to play as police
  • You lead the police. Protester crowds are AI: they gather people and march toward the কেন্দ্রীয় শহীদ মিনার. You are faster than any crowd, so run them down.
  • Win by dispersing 10 crowds. Lose if a crowd of 15 holds the Minar for 10 seconds, or if public anger reaches 100.
  • Recruit officers: walk into the blue officers on the streets and they join your squad. A bigger squad scatters more people per hit.
  • Strike (click or Space) scatters people close to you. Smoke grenade (G) makes a cloud that slows and breaks up crowds. Warning shots (F) scatter people in front of you. Nobody is hurt: people just run away.
  • Using force raises public anger, and angry crowds grow faster. Anger falls slowly when you hold back. Smoke and rounds come back over time.
  • On a phone, use the round pad to walk and the STRIKE, SMOKE and FIRE buttons on the left.

দেশি আন্দোলন

Deshi Andolon is a single-player march game. Gather a crowd in white, get through the police barricades, and hold the কেন্দ্রীয় শহীদ মিনার (Central Shaheed Minar) with 15 people for 10 seconds.

  • Move the mouse to walk. Walk into bystanders to bring them along.
  • Police barricades (6 on the outer ring, 2 on each inner ring) open when your crowd is big enough: 6, then 14, then 25 people. Stand near one and wait.
  • Police (blue) carry sticks, with just a few in the outer zones and more near the centre. Each hit costs you morale. If they hit you when your morale is nearly gone, a police van takes you to a thana or Aynaghor and the game is over.
  • Hospitals restore your morale after a beating. বিরিয়ানি stalls restore morale too, and মামার টঙের চা gives a quick boost.
  • Rivals (black clothes) support the police and fight your crowd when you meet. The bigger crowd wins the clash, and a tie goes to the protesters. Expect gunshot and blast sounds during fights.
  • Buildings are solid, so walk around them on the roads.
  • The date runs from 1 July to 36 July. Press M to mute sound.
Fictional game. Characters and events are not real people or incidents. Map is stylised.

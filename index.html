Andolon is a single-player game. You can play as an Andolonkari (protester) or as a Police officer. Select a character and proceed.

Goal: gather a crowd in white, get through the police barricades, and hold the কেন্দ্রীয় শহীদ মিনার (Central Shaheed Minar) with 15 people for 10 seconds.

How to play as an Andolonkari

  • Move the mouse to walk. On a phone, touch and hold a direction on the round pad at the bottom right. Walk into bystanders to bring them along.
  • Police barricades (6 on the outer ring, 2 on each inner ring) open when your crowd is big enough: 6, then 14, then 25 people. Stand near one and wait.
  • Police (blue) carry sticks. Each hit costs you morale. If they hit you when your morale is nearly gone, a police van takes you to a thana or Aynaghor and the game is over.
  • Hospitals restore your morale after a beating. বিরিয়ানি stalls restore morale too, and মামার টঙের চা gives a quick boost.
  • Rivals (black clothes) support the police and fight your crowd when you meet. The bigger crowd wins, and a tie goes to the protesters. A bigger rival crowd standing in the square stops you holding it.
  • Buildings are solid, so walk around them on the roads.
  • You have 30 days (about 4 minutes). Your score comes from crowd size, barricades passed, fights won and speed. Press M (or tap the speaker) to mute sound.
Goal: stop the protester crowds. Disperse 10 crowds before one of them holds the কেন্দ্রীয় শহীদ মিনার (15 people for 10 seconds), and keep public anger below 100.

How to play as Police

  • The protesters are AI. They gather people and march toward the Minar. You are faster than any crowd and can walk through barricades, so run them down.
  • Move the mouse to walk. On a phone, use the round pad at the bottom right.
  • Recruit officers: walk into the blue officers on the streets and they join your squad (up to 14). A bigger squad scatters more people per hit.
  • Strike (click or Space, or the STRIKE button) scatters people close to you. Smoke grenade (G or SMOKE) makes a cloud that slows and breaks up crowds. Warning shots (F or FIRE) scatter people in front of you. Nobody is hurt: people just run away. Smoke and rounds come back over time.
  • A crowd with nobody left is dispersed after a couple more hits or a few seconds in smoke.
  • Using force raises public anger, and angry crowds grow faster. Anger falls slowly when you hold back. If it reaches 100 you are pulled back and the game is over.
  • Two crowds that meet join into a bigger one, so break them up early. Press M (or tap the speaker) to mute sound.
